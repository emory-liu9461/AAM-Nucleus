# Credence brand

Parent brand **Credence** with trade brands: Painting, Cleaning, Roofing, Renovations, Concrete, Landscaping.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `credence[-trade].svg` | Horizontal logo for light backgrounds (website header, invoices, email) |
| `credence[-trade]-white.svg` | Horizontal logo for dark backgrounds or over photos (truck wraps, yard signs, dark ads) |
| `credence[-trade]-stacked.svg` | Stacked logo for square spaces (shirts, flyers, social cover) |
| `credence[-trade]-mark.svg` | Badge only: social profile pictures, app/browser icons, stickers |

**Colours**
- Credence Navy `#14213D` · Credence Gold `#C9A227` (parent brand check)
- Painting `#0F8B8D` (on dark `#3CC4C6`) · Cleaning `#2E9E5B` (`#5FD18C`) · Roofing `#B5452F` (`#E57A63`)
- Renovations `#B8730A` (`#E9A63F`) · Concrete `#5B6B7F` (`#9FB0C4`) · Landscaping `#4C8C2B` (`#7FC25A`)

**Type:** Montserrat Bold (CREDENCE) and SemiBold (trade name), converted to outlines. Montserrat is licensed
under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, so it's free for commercial use. Use Montserrat for headlines on print and ads to match.

**The mark:** a "C" with a check leaving its opening, meaning trust that's been verified. Keep clear space around
the logo of at least the badge's corner radius, and don't recolour, stretch or add effects.

These are original designs. Before registering the name or printing at scale, search the Canadian trademark
database (CIPO) and your provincial business registry for "Credence" in your trades.

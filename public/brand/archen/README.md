# archen. brand

Parent brand **archen.** with trade brands: Construction, Painting, Cleaning, Roofing, Renovations, Concrete, Landscaping.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `archen[-trade].svg` | Horizontal logo for light backgrounds (website header, invoices, email) |
| `archen[-trade]-white.svg` | Horizontal logo for dark backgrounds or over photos (truck wraps, yard signs, dark ads) |
| `archen[-trade]-stacked.svg` | Stacked logo for narrower spaces (shirts, flyers, social covers) |
| `archen[-trade]-mark.svg` | Square "a." icon: social profile pictures, browser tab and app icons, stickers |

**Colours**
- Archen Navy `#14213D` · Archen Gold `#C9A227` (on dark `#E2BE4A`): the parent brand's dot.
- Construction `#2F6DB5` (on dark `#6FA3E6`) · Painting `#0F8B8D` (`#3CC4C6`) · Cleaning `#2E9E5B` (`#5FD18C`)
- Roofing `#B5452F` (`#E57A63`) · Renovations `#B8730A` (`#E9A63F`) · Concrete `#5B6B7F` (`#9FB0C4`) · Landscaping `#4C8C2B` (`#7FC25A`)

**Type:** Outfit Bold ("archen") and Outfit Light (the trade), all lowercase, converted to outlines. Outfit is licensed
under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, so it's free for commercial use. Use Outfit for headlines on print and ads to match.

**Rules:** always lowercase; the dot is always the trade's colour (gold for the parent brand) and never left out.
Keep clear space around the logo at least the height of the "a", and don't recolour, stretch or add effects.

These are original designs. Before registering the name or printing at scale, search the Canadian trademark
database (CIPO) and the Ontario business registry for "Archen" in your trades.

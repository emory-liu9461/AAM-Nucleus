# Arcus Construction logo

*Arcus* is Latin for "arch". The mark is a stone arch whose keystone, the stone that locks every other stone
in place, is picked out in amber: solid, lasting work held together by trust.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `arcus-construction.svg` | Horizontal logo for light backgrounds (website header, invoices, email) |
| `arcus-construction-white.svg` | Horizontal logo for dark backgrounds (truck wraps, signs, dark ads) |
| `arcus-construction-stacked[-white].svg` | Stacked logo for square spaces (shirts, hard hats, flyers, social covers) |
| `arcus-construction-mark.svg` | Square app icon: social profile pictures, browser tab |
| `arcus-construction-symbol.svg` | The arch alone, no background: stickers, embroidery, site signage |

**Colours:** Slate `#1C2B3A` · Keystone Amber `#D9822B` (on dark backgrounds `#F0A24E`).

**Type:** Montserrat Bold (ARCUS) and SemiBold (CONSTRUCTION), widely letter-spaced and converted to outlines.
Montserrat is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, so it's free for commercial use.

**Rules:** the keystone is always amber; keep clear space around the logo at least the width of one pier;
don't recolour, stretch or add effects.

Before registering the name or printing at scale, search the Canadian trademark database (CIPO) and the
Ontario business registry for "Arcus" in construction.

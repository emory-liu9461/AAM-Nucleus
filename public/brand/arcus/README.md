# Arcus brand

Parent brand **Arcus** with trade brands: Construction, Painting, Cleaning, Roofing, Renovations, Concrete, Landscaping.

*Arcus* is Latin for "arch". The mark is a stone arch whose keystone, the stone that locks every other stone in
place, is picked out in colour: solid, lasting work held together by trust. Each trade's keystone and trade name
take that trade's colour; the parent brand's keystone is amber.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `arcus[-trade].svg` | Horizontal logo for light backgrounds (website header, invoices, email) |
| `arcus[-trade]-white.svg` | Horizontal logo for dark backgrounds (truck wraps, signs, dark ads) |
| `arcus[-trade]-stacked.svg` | Stacked logo for square spaces (shirts, hard hats, flyers, social covers) |
| `arcus[-trade]-mark.svg` | Square icon: social profile pictures, browser tab and app icons, stickers |

**Colours**
- Arcus Slate `#1C2B3A` · Keystone Amber `#D9822B` (on dark `#F0A24E`): parent brand and Construction
- Painting `#0F8B8D` (on dark `#3CC4C6`) · Cleaning `#2E9E5B` (`#5FD18C`) · Roofing `#B5452F` (`#E57A63`)
- Renovations `#7A5BA6` (`#B394E0`) · Concrete `#5B6B7F` (`#9FB0C4`) · Landscaping `#4C8C2B` (`#7FC25A`)

**Type:** Montserrat Bold (ARCUS) and SemiBold (trade name), widely letter-spaced and converted to outlines.
Montserrat is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, so it's free for commercial use.

**Rules:** the keystone is always the trade's colour; keep clear space around the logo at least the width of one
pier; don't recolour, stretch or add effects.

Before registering the name or printing at scale, search the Canadian trademark database (CIPO) and the
Ontario business registry for "Arcus" in your trades.
